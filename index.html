How we work

A single process, from scope to handover.

01

Scope & site survey

+
We assess the site, confirm scale and specification with your project team, and issue a scoped proposal within two business days.
02

Procurement & sourcing

+
Vendor tendering and direct-factory sourcing run against your BOM, benchmarked across our regional supplier network.
03

Manufacturing & fit-out

+
In-house joinery and MEP-coordinated fit-out run in parallel, tracked against a single critical path.
04

Handover & snagging

+
Final inspection, snagging, and occupancy or opening-day readiness sign-off, with a single point of accountability throughout.
